# Cascade: Colour Sorting — Support

Need help? Email **biszaalgames@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you.

## How to play

- Every lane should end up holding a single colour.
- Tap a lane to lift its top token, then tap another lane to drop it there.
- A token can only land on an empty lane, or on a token of the same colour.
- One tap-pair moves exactly one token. The fewer moves you use, the more stars you earn.

## Special tokens

- **Face-down tokens** show "?" until the token above them is moved away.
- **Anchored tokens** sit at the bottom of a lane and never move, so that lane can only ever
  finish in the anchor's colour.

## Common questions

**Is my progress saved?** Yes, on your device, after every level you solve.

**Can I move my progress to a new phone?** Not yet — progress is stored only on the device you
play on.

**How do I start over?** Settings → Reset everything. This cannot be undone.

**Do I need an internet connection?** No. Every level, including the daily challenge, works
fully offline.

**Does the game have ads or in-app purchases?** No.

**Is it accessible with VoiceOver?** Yes. Every lane describes what it holds and what tapping
it will do.
