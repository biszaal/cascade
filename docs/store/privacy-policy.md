# Privacy Policy — Cascade: Colour Sorting

**Effective date:** 14 September 2026
**Developer:** Biszaal Tech Ltd.
**Contact:** biszaalgames@gmail.com

The published policy is <https://www.biszaaltech.com/games/cascade/privacy>. This file mirrors it
for the repository; if the two ever disagree, the published page is the one players see.

## The short version

Cascade creates an anonymous account so your results can be backed up and entered on the daily
leaderboard. There is no sign-up, no email, no name you enter, no advertising, no analytics, and
no tracking.

## What is collected

- **An anonymous account**, created the first time the game opens with a connection. It holds no
  email address, name, or phone number.
- **A generated player name** such as "Player 1234", shown beside your result on the daily
  leaderboard. You don't choose it.
- **Level results**: for each level you solve, your best number of moves, star rating, and
  fastest time.
- **Daily results**: your number of moves and time for that day's puzzle.

Results finished offline stay on your device and are sent when the game next connects. Your
settings and hints never leave your device.

## Who processes it

Supabase hosts the accounts, results, and leaderboard. The database is in Ireland. Nothing else
leaves the game: no advertising, analytics, or tracking services, and no in-app purchases.

## Who can see it

Each account can read only its own results. The daily leaderboard shows the day's top 100 —
player name, moves, and time — to players of the game.

## Keeping and deleting it

Your account and results are kept until you delete them. In the game, open **Settings › Delete my
data**: it deletes your anonymous account, your level and daily results, and your progress on the
device. You can also email **biszaalgames@gmail.com** with the player name shown for you on the
daily leaderboard, and we will delete them for you. Deleting the game loses the account on your
device, and reinstalling starts a new one.

## Children

Cascade is not directed to children under 13. A player never enters any information: the account
is anonymous and its name is generated.

## Changes

We update this policy before any version that changes what is collected.
