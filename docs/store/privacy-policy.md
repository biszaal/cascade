# Privacy Policy — Cascade: Colour Sorting

**Effective date:** 13 September 2026
**Developer:** Biszaal Tech Ltd.
**Contact:** biszaalgames@gmail.com

## The short version

Cascade does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There is no account,
no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

The game saves the following **only on your device**, so you can pick up where you left off:

- which levels you have solved, your star ratings, and your best move counts
- your settings (sound and haptics)
- how many hints you have left

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. Deleting the app, or using
**Settings → Reset everything**, removes it.

## What we collect

Nothing. This version of Cascade makes no network requests on your behalf and contains no
third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

## Children

Cascade is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects
none from children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version adds online features — such as a daily leaderboard or syncing progress
between devices — this policy will be updated **before** that version is released, and it
will describe exactly what is sent and why.
